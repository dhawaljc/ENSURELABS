Creating Science for a Built Environment That Endures

Our vision

At ENSURE Lab, we investigate how the built environment interacts with natural, technological, and human systems throughout its life cycle. By integrating environmental life cycle assessment, artificial intelligence, multi-objective optimization, resilience science, and immersive technologies, we develop predictive frameworks and science-based solutions that reduce environmental impacts, improve building performance, and strengthen resilience to a changing climate.

Our vision is to advance a built environment that is environmentally responsible, climate-resilient, and adaptive to the changing needs of people and the planet.

Our science

Five interconnected research themes underpin our vision of a sustainable and resilient built environment, connecting scientific discovery, predictive intelligence, and transformative innovation to address present challenges and anticipate future needs.

  • Assess: What are the environmental consequences? Quantifying whole-life environmental performance across materials, processes, buildings, and infrastructure. We develop comprehensive life cycle assessment frameworks that quantify environmental impacts from raw material extraction through end-of-life recovery. By integrating embodied and operational impacts within an energy-carbon-water nexus, we provide science-based evidence that informs sustainable decisions spanning construction materials, building systems, whole buildings, and communities.
  • Optimize: How do we balance competing objectives? Identifying optimal design and retrofit strategies for evolving environmental and technological futures. Decisions made today determine environmental performance for decades. ENSURE Lab develops forward and inverse multi-objective optimization frameworks that balance environmental impacts, cost, durability, occupant well-being, and resilience while accounting for future climate scenarios, electricity grid decarbonization, technology transitions, and resource availability.
  • Predict: How will buildings perform in the future? Transforming complex simulations into predictive intelligence for engineering decisions. We develop trustworthy, physics- and economics-informed artificial intelligence to predict building performance across diverse design and future operating conditions. Integrating machine learning, digital twins, high-fidelity simulations, and uncertainty quantification, our research accelerates performance prediction and enables informed decision-making while preserving scientific rigor.
  • Transform: How can we build to adapt to a changing world? Creating climate-resilient and regenerative built environments. Environmental sustainability alone is no longer sufficient. We help develop bio-based and bio-inspired materials, climate-responsive building systems, and integrated resilience assessment methods to strengthen building performance under future hazards while minimizing life cycle environmental impacts. Through community-engaged research and immersive technologies, we translate scientific knowledge into practical solutions for sustainable, resilient, and regenerative built environments.
  • Educate: How can we make the invisible visible? Transforming complex environmental science into immersive, experiential learning and informed action. We develop immersive learning environments using virtual, augmented, and mixed reality technologies to make complex environmental processes, life cycle impacts, and climate risks tangible and accessible. By integrating interactive simulations, experiential learning, and community engagement, we advance environmental literacy, strengthen climate preparedness, and empower learners, professionals, and communities to make informed decisions for a sustainable and resilient future.
