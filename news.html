Award
2024
Maryam Abbasi Kamazani wins Best Paper Award at the International Conference on Net Zero Carbon Built Environment

Ph.D. Candidate Maryam Abbasi Kamazani received the Best Paper Award for her research on climate-aware multi-objective optimization of building envelope performance. The paper presented a prognostic framework jointly optimizing the energy–carbon–water nexus under future climate scenarios, funded by the National Science Foundation.
Recent News
Funding
Active Grant · National Science Foundation
NSF funds prognostic multi-objective optimization of building energy–carbon–water nexus
This grant supports development of a prognostic optimization framework that anticipates non-stationary climate and energy system conditions across all U.S. sub-climate zones. Led by Prof. Dixit with Ph.D. Candidate Maryam Abbasi Kamazani.
Funding
Active Grant · DOE ARPA-E
DOE ARPA-E funds LCA of 3D-printed hempcrete walls for large-scale additive construction
Ph.D. Candidate Yasaman Norouzi is conducting a cradle-to-grave life cycle assessment of hempcrete for extrusion-based large-scale 3D printing as part of this collaborative DOE project.
Collaboration
Ongoing · New Frontiers in Research Fund
7-country Living Lab collaboration on climate resilience spans three continents
Our multinational collaboration with partners in the US, Canada, Norway, UK, Switzerland, Indonesia, and Colombia is co-designing resilience solutions with communities in Port Arthur TX, Medellín Colombia, and Indramayu Indonesia.
Research
2024 · NSF-Funded
NSF-funded collaboration with Penn State examines hygrothermal climate stress on building envelopes
ENSURE Lab and Penn State are jointly studying how climate-driven temperature and humidity swings degrade building envelope performance over 100+ year life cycles, integrating CMIP6 projections and CORDEX downscaled data.
