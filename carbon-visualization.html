The atmosphere
doesn't forget

CO₂ emitted today will warm the planet for hundreds of years. Atmospheric concentration reached 422.8 ppm in 2024, the highest in over 3 million years, 52% above pre-industrial levels. At current rates, the remaining 1.5°C carbon budget runs out within five years.
