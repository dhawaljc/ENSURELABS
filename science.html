Every brick spends energy
C1 · Life Cycle Assessment & Environmental Impacts

Matter matters.

Every material in a building carries a hidden carbon cost: in the mines, the factories, the trucks, and the landfills. Most of the world counts only the energy a building uses while occupied. We count everything, from raw extraction to end-of-life demolition.

A city exhaling CO₂
D1 · Sustainability-Resilience Nexus & Living Labs

Resilience is personal.

Port Arthur has flooded repeatedly. Medellín has faced decades of instability. Indramayu watches the sea rise. We don't design solutions for these communities: we design with them, in Living Labs that put residents at the centre of every decision.

The Pareto front · one design chosen
E1 · Climate-Aware Design & Optimization

Buildings outlive their blueprints.

A building designed for today's climate will live through tomorrow's. We optimise buildings not for the world they're born into but for the world they'll age through, jointly minimising energy, carbon and water across every future climate scenario we can model.

Holding the building’s future
F1 · Human-Centered Immersive Training & Simulation

See it to build it.

Understanding how a building performs, or how to construct one safely, is hard to teach from a textbook. So we put people inside virtual buildings, virtual construction sites and virtual extreme environments instead.
